# Spring Roll Bowls

## Ingredients

- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- ¼ cup agave or brown sugar
- ¼ cup fish sauce
- ⅓ cup lime juice
- ⅓ cup vegetable oil
- rice noodles, basil, mint, and cilantro, serrano peppers, chopped peanuts, avocado, veggies (carrots, bell peppers, cucumbers), protein (shrimp or chicken)
- all the fresh produce can get expensive

## Instructions

Cook noodles for 3 minutes. Chop veggies and herbs. Cook shrimp (if desired) and mix sauce. Mix all together in a bowl and top with peanuts and avocado.
