# Homemade Biscuits

> Some details are still in the original Google Doc (see `source`). Fill them in here when you can.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp granulated sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 6 tbsp unsalted butter (very cold
- European butter ideal)
- ¾ cup whole milk

## Instructions

In Doc, see link for full instructions. Mix dry ingredients, cut in cold butter, add milk, fold and cut biscuits, bake.
