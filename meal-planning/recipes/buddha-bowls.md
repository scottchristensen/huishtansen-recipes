# Buddha Bowls

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp oil
- ½ red onion (wedges)
- 2 small sweet potatoes (halved)
- 1 bundle broccolini
- 2 handfuls kale, salt + pepper
- 1 can chickpeas
- 1 tsp cumin
- ¾ tsp chili powder
- ¾ tsp garlic powder, salt + pepper
- ½ tsp oregano
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- ¼ cup tahini
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- ½ lemon (juiced)
- 2-4 tbsp hot water

## Instructions

Roast veggies and chickpeas in oven. Cover with tahini dressing.
