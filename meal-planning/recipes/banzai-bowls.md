# Banzai Bowls

## Ingredients

- 1 packet acai
- ½ cup frozen strawberries
- ½ cup frozen mango
- ⅔ frozen banana
- 4 oz apple juice (or hemp milk)
- granola (preferably Nature's Path Hemp Hearts), shredded coconut, banana slices, strawberry slices, blueberries, kiwi slices, honey

## Instructions

Blend all acai base ingredients. Pour into large container. Add toppings and enjoy!
