# Peach Prosciutto Italian Salad

## Ingredients

- drizzle of olive oil, drizzle of balsamic, salt and pepper
- 2 big handfuls arugula
- ½ cup cherry tomatoes (halved) or peaches if in season
- 1 ball burrata cheese (ripped)
- 5 basil leaves (chopped)
- 4-6 pieces prosciutto (ripped)

## Instructions

Rip burrata and prosciutto, cut tomatoes or peaches and toss everything together. Voila!
