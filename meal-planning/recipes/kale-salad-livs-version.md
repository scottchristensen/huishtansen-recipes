# Kale Salad (Liv's Version)

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup olive oil
- ⅓ cup lemon juice
- 2 tsp sugar or honey
- 2 tsp garlic paste
- 1 big bunch kale (stripped and chopped)
- ½ cup grated parm
- ½ red onion (thinly sliced)
- 1 pear/apple (thinly sliced or diced)
- ½ cup cranberries or chopped dates
- ½ cup candied almonds
- ¼ cup hemp seeds

## Instructions

Chop kale, make dressing, and toss everything together.
