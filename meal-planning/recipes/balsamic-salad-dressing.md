# Balsamic Salad Dressing

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
- ¼ cup olive oil
- 2 heaping tsp Dijon mustard
- 2 tsp sugar
- ½ tsp salt

## Instructions

Whisk together all ingredients.
