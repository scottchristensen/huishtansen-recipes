# Salmon with Mango Salsa

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs fresh salmon
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 mangoes (diced)
- 1 avocado (diced)
- ¼ cup cilantro (minced)
- ¼ cup red onion (minced), jalapeño (optional), drizzle of honey, squeeze of lime juice + zest, salt to taste

## Instructions

Season salmon, cook in air fryer or oven, prepare fresh mango salsa, serve together.
